Why patients come to Arvis

Allergy at the coreArvis began as an allergy and immunology clinic, and that is still what we know best — from simple hay fever to the cases other clinics have given up on. Experienced allergists, up to 35 years of practiceAdults and children. Consultation lasts 30 minutes because a proper allergy history cannot be taken in ten. Advanced tests we run on siteWe perform the basophil activation test — the method that settles drug and venom allergy when history and standard tests disagree. Treatment of the cause, not only the symptomAllergen immunotherapy, and autolymphocytotherapy — a patented Russian method (RU 2395305) that we are the only clinic in the Black Earth region licensed to perform.

We also do not prescribe “just in case”. Which tests you actually need is decided at the consultation, after the history and the examination — not from a list on a website.

Care in your language

Some of our allergists consult in English. They are not on duty every day, so please tell us in advance. Write on WhatsApp or to info@arvisclinic.ru, say that you need an English-speaking doctor and give two or three convenient dates — we will confirm a slot with a doctor who speaks English. If your case needs a full medical interpreter, we will tell you honestly rather than let you find out at the door.

We also have a member of staff who communicates in sign language, so deaf and hard-of-hearing patients can be seen without bringing an interpreter. As with English, please arrange it in advance.

Diagnostics your doctor at home will understand

This matters most if you plan to return to your country or move on. We work with internationally standardised platforms, so your report is not a local document that nobody abroad can read.

ImmunoCAP (Thermo Fisher / Phadia)The reference method for specific IgE. Results in kUA/L on the same scale used across Europe and the US. Single allergens from 980 ₽, Phadiatop inhalant screening 3 930 ₽. ALEX² and ALEX³ molecular arraysOver 300 allergens and components from one blood draw, including alpha-gal in ALEX³. Shows which protein exactly you react to and predicts cross-reactions. от 23 500 ₽ and 25 290 ₽. Skin prick testsThe classic in-office method, read in 15–20 minutes. 5 allergens 2 500 ₽, up to 20 allergens 5 500 ₽. Not performed during pollen season or while you take antihistamines. Basophil activation testUsed when the history and the standard tests disagree — drug and insect venom allergy in particular. From от 5 100 ₽.

Your results come as a printed report and a PDF stating the method, the units and the reference ranges, so an allergist in another country can interpret them without repeating the tests. Ask us at the visit if you need the report in English.

Before you come

Do I need to speak Russian?
Not for the consultation itself, provided you arranged an English-speaking doctor in advance. Reception staff work in Russian, so writing to us on WhatsApp or by email first is the easiest route.
What should I bring?
A passport, and any previous allergy results even if they are in your own language — allergen codes and units are international.
How do I pay?
Russian bank cards and cash. Cards issued outside Russia do not work here, so please plan for cash.
Can I be tested during pollen season?
Blood tests — yes, any day, including at the height of the season. Skin prick tests are postponed until the season ends and antihistamines are stopped.
Will the results be valid in my country?
ImmunoCAP and ALEX are used worldwide and the report states the method, units and reference values, so your doctor at home reads it as a standard laboratory result.
How long does it take to get results?
Single IgE tests a few working days; the large molecular panels up to two weeks. We send them electronically as soon as the laboratory releases them.
